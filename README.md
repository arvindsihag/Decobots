# Decobots

Decolonizing Cobots: An SDG-Aligned Framework for Equitable Robotic Integration

## Overview

Decobots is a research and outreach project focused on rethinking the role of collaborative robots (cobots) through a decolonial, equity-centered lens. The project explores how robotic systems can be designed, evaluated, and deployed in ways that are aligned with social justice, local context, and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Rather than treating automation as a purely technical or efficiency-driven intervention, this framework asks how robotics can support communities, reduce inequities, and promote sustainable and inclusive innovation.

## Why this project matters

The repository and accompanying web materials position robotics as a socio-technical practice that must account for:

- community agency and local knowledge
- ethical and equitable deployment of automation
- labor, education, and accessibility considerations
- sustainability and responsible production
- cross-sector partnerships and public impact

## SDG alignment

The project explicitly connects its framework to the following SDG themes:

- Quality Education
- Decent Work and Economic Growth
- Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
- Reduced Inequalities
- Responsible Consumption and Production
- Partnerships for the Goals
